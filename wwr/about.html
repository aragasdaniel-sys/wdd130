Whitewater Rafting

Our mission is to share the thrill of the river safely and responsibly. Whether it's your first rapid or your hundredth, our guides will help you leave with a great story and a big smile.

Whitewater Rafting began in 2008 when two friends, both of them river guides, decided to share their favorite adventures with everyone. What started with one raft and a borrowed trailer quickly grew into a trusted local name.

History

Today we guide thousands of guests each season, from first-time families to experienced paddlers. Every trip is led by certified guides who put safety first and make sure each trip is a great one.
